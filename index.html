    About

    Why Reminisce?

    Our story

    Reminisce began on a slow afternoon in a small kitchen, frying what smelled like childhood. Every recipe here is someone's memory — a grandmother's curry, a mother's sweets — kept alive one plate at a time.

    Our mission

    To give every home a dish that carries it back to another one. We cook with the flavors we grew up on, so a single bite can feel like arriving somewhere you belong.

    Problem

    The gap we close

    The problem

    Busy days pull us away from the table. Meals become quick and forgettable, and the flavors of home slip out of reach — too far, too hard, too time-consuming to find again.

    Our solution

    Reminisce captures those flavors and brings them to you — authentic family recipes, cooked the way they've always been cooked, and delivered while the warmth is still in them.
